# Camion

> Camion is an infrastructure-intelligence platform accelerating the electrification of real estate portfolios. Its AI-driven platform indexes properties by readiness and potential for electrification, forecasts energy demand, and optimizes placement of solar, storage, and EV-charging infrastructure so real estate owners and infrastructure developers can underwrite and deploy energy upgrades at scale. Founded 2023, London. Backed by EQT Ventures (EUR 2.7M pre-seed, April 2024). Sector: energy-and-environmental.
